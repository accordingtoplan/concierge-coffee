### 7. Shipping: whole US, same day, flat rate

Set in the Shopify admin, not in the repo. Decided: one shipping zone, United States, flat rate, orders placed before a cutoff ship the same day. Benjamin bags and ships; he is buying mailer boxes.

How the flat rate gets its number: Shopify Basic includes Shopify Shipping (discounted USPS and UPS labels bought inside the order screen, no carrier account needed). Once Benjamin has a box, weigh one 300g bag packed and one 1kg bag packed, enter the weights on the variants, and read the USPS Ground Advantage label price from Shopify Shipping for a Los Angeles to New York address and a Los Angeles to San Francisco address. The flat rate is roughly the average of the two for a single 300g bag; round to a clean number and consider free shipping above a threshold that covers two or three bags. Set the same-day cutoff in the shipping policy copy on the product page and in the order confirmation email (something like "ordered before 12 pm Pacific, ships the same day"); do not promise it in the hero.

Also enable **local pickup** at 821 Traction as a Shopify pickup rate for bags. It costs nothing and it is the free option for LA customers.

Before launch: place a test order to a friend's address in another state and confirm the shipping step shows the flat rate, the label can be bought, and the confirmation email reads right.
